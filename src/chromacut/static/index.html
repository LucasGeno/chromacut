Clean icons out of green-screen art.

Drop an AI-generated chroma-key sheet. Chromacut finds the grid, lifts each piece off its background, and hands you ready-to-use transparent PNGs.

01

Drop the sheet

The key color and grid are detected automatically. Nudge the boxes if you want them tighter.

03

Export clean PNGs

Edges are despilled and padded, then exported as transparent PNGs — one file or a zip.
