# General Tips

## Why #00FF00?

Solid bright green (#00FF00) provides the maximum color distance from most subject colors, making extraction reliable.

## Grid Layout

- Leave at least 80px of solid green between icons
- Labels go on a black strip below the content, never overlapping

## What to Avoid

- Green in your artwork (use sage #8B9B6B or olive #6B7B4B instead)
- Anti-aliasing on pixel art (request "NO anti-aliasing" in your prompt)
- Gradients that touch the key color
